About the Atelier

Furniture built with the precision of an engineer, the eye of a designer.

Since 2009, AF Steel Furniture has fabricated bespoke metal furniture and steel accessories for architects, interior designers, and homeowners who refuse to compromise. Every piece is made in our Cape Town workshop by skilled craftsmen who understand that quality is in the details.

We design and manufacture every possible steel furniture piece and accessory — no shortcuts, no flatpack, no compromise. The result is furniture and details that don't just look exceptional but outlasts the buildings it lives in.

How We Work

The process

01

Consultation

We work closely with you to understand your space, requirements, and aesthetic vision. Every project begins with a conversation.

02

Design & Engineering

Our engineers produce precise technical drawings. Every millimetre is accounted for before a single sheet of steel is cut.

03

Fabrication

Skilled craftsmen cut, weld, and finish each piece by hand in our Cape Town workshop using heavy-gauge cold-rolled steel.

04

Finishing & Delivery

Powder-coated to your specification and delivered white-glove to your door. We don't leave until it's installed to your satisfaction.
